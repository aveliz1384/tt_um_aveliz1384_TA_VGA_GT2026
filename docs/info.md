## How it works
This project generates a VGA video signal that displays vertical RGB (Red, Green, and Blue) color bars on a monitor.

The digital circuit generates the necessary VGA synchronization signals and controls the RGB outputs to produce vertical color bars across the screen.

## How to test
1. Connect a VGA-compatible monitor to the chip's VGA output pins using the appropriate VGA connector and wiring.

2. Power on the chip and the monitor.

3. Verify that vertical RGB (Red, Green, and Blue) color bars appear on the screen.

4. Check that the colors are displayed correctly and that the image remains stable.
